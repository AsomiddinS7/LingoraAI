# 2-minute demo script

Setup: Chrome on a phone or laptop, preview link open, Console set to Plan B with mock on.

1. **0:00 Sign to text.** Tap *Turn on camera* and then *Start*. Point out "Video stays on this device".
2. **0:15 Low confidence.** Wait for "Not recognized. Try again or choose a phrase". The confidence is written as text, not shown by color only.
3. **0:30 Confirm a sign.** When a sign is detected, tap *Use this sign* or *Add another sign*. A sentence comes back. Show that nothing is spoken until you tap *Accept and read aloud*.
4. **0:50 Reply.** Open *Text to speech → Their reply*, type a reply and tap *Show to signer*. The reply appears in large text. Tap *Simplify*.
5. **1:05 Failure states.** In Console, set the fixture to *Upstream error*, then sign again. Show the error and the *Use signs as words* fallback. Then show *Refusal*, then *Offline* (the phrasebook still works).
6. **1:30 Plan A.** Tap *Apply Plan A*. Hold-to-talk appears with the privacy notice. Turn on a face hint. No code change was needed.
7. **1:45 Safety.** Show the *Ask for a human interpreter* button, the About screen (situations where the tool must not be used, supported signs) and the switch between three languages.
